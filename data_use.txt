HMP2 metagenomic samples

Obtain the two HMP2 files listed in dataset_urls.txt. read_hmp2_metagenomics(metadata_path, profile_path) reads the official CSV and gzipped taxonomic table, joins samples by their original identifier, selects species-level features, and retains subject, site, disease, and sampling week. by_subject returns times in days and feature vectors. centered_log_ratio requires an explicitly selected positive pseudocount; the paper does not specify this value.

HMP2MetagenomicPairs requires an explicit set of training subjects, a pseudocount, the number of days per model time unit, and a maximum pair interval. assert_subject_disjoint checks user-supplied partitions. The paper's flare holdout and site-stratified cross-validation require cohort annotations and have not been constructed here. model_for_metagenomic_prototype connects species features to the existing simplified trainable oscillator. This prototype represents only metagenomics and is not the paper's complete nine-modality model.

TCGA

read_gdc_project verifies a downloaded project response. read_star_counts reads an official GDC STAR count table with an explicitly selected strand column. methylation_m_values and copy_number_log_ratio provide the mathematical transforms for numerical arrays; clipping and reference arrays must be specified explicitly and the reference must be determined from the training partition. RNA variance stabilization and the complete TCGA data assembly are not implemented.

GEO

read_geo_soft verifies the actual accession, series title, and sample listing. filter_and_normalize_counts requires raw counts, mitochondrial-gene flags, doublet flags, and an explicit normalization target; it rejects cells with at least 20 percent mitochondrial counts. compartment_frequencies uses existing cell annotations and never invents them. read_geo_processed_csv streams selected genes from a processed gene-by-cell CSV and preserves negative values; it does not treat processed values as raw counts. Loading full raw count matrices, doublet detection, reference mapping, and pseudotime are not implemented.

GSE150115 describes five ulcerative colitis samples, not the paper's Crohn cohort. GSE181919 is head and neck cancer, not colorectal cancer. require_paper_compatible refuses both for their paper-assigned roles. GSE116222 is epithelial-focused; its metadata alone cannot establish immune and stromal cell coverage.
